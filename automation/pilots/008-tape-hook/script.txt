If the hook on your tape measure wiggles, do not tighten it. That movement is the calibration.

When you hook the tape over an outside edge and pull, the hook slides outward. The measurement begins from its inner face. But when you push the tape against a wall for an inside measurement, the hook slides inward and its own metal thickness becomes part of the measured distance.

The slots around the rivets allow the hook to move by approximately that thickness. That way, both pulling from an edge and pushing against a surface can start at the same zero point. If the hook were fixed, one of those measurements would be off.

So the loose part is not bad assembly. It is a tiny mechanical correction built into the tool.
