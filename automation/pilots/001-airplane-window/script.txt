That tiny hole in your airplane window is not a leak. It decides which pane takes the pressure.

Passenger windows use multiple layers. The panel you can touch mainly protects the structural panes from scratches. Behind it, the small breather hole lets cabin air enter the gap between the middle and outer panes.

That equalizes pressure on both sides of the middle pane, so the outer pane normally carries the pressure difference between the cabin and the thin air outside. If the outer pane is damaged, the middle pane is strong enough to act as a backup. The airflow also helps move moisture away, reducing fog.

So the hole is not a weakness. It is pressure management built into the window.
